\section{Falsifiers and Reproducibility Template}
\label{app:falsifiers}
The following are counterexamples to the intended contracts, not observations reported by this study:
\begin{itemize}
\item A model-controlled field creates a trusted principal or broadens a grant without trusted admission.
\item An ordinary child effect exceeds the parent's effective scope under the same resource interpretation, or a trusted read-view exception grants write, execution, or network authority.
\item A resumed cycle restores revoked authority, or a stale live-worker lease holder commits an effect or checkpoint.
\item A backend reports full enforcement when a required mechanism is absent or only partially effective.
\item A content-identical renamed artifact counts as novel progress, or a completion proposal is silently accepted as the human goal.
\item An uncertain side effect is repeated without reconciliation, or a finding alone triggers privileged enforcement.
\end{itemize}
